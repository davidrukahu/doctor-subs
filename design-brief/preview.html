Very first visit to the plugin. No scan has run yet. Calm CTA + bulleted detect list. The moment that has to lower a panicking merchant's heart rate.

After the user clicks Scan my subscriptions. Deterministic progress bar with live sub_id being checked, not a spinner. Honest not performative.

Store has no active subs yet. Alternative copy, no CTA, doesn't pretend there's work to do.
Surface 2 · Dashboard
Default (mixed)
The normal state after a scan. Three counter cards up top (click to filter), “Needs attention” table below. Terracotta for broken, warm amber for at-risk, muted green for healthy.
Surface 2 · Dashboard
All healthy
Zero broken, zero at-risk. Table is replaced by a quiet confirmation, no confetti, no emoji. The most calming state the product can produce.
Surface 2 · Dashboard
Scan failed
Scanner crashed or timed out. Inline banner above the counters — calm tone, action available, nothing blocking the rest of the UI.
Known issue (flagged during design review): the banner uses border-left: 2px solid, which is on Anthropic's frontend-design skill's banned patterns list (“side-stripe borders on cards/alerts”). Acceptable on the counter selection state (precedent in Notion/Linear), debatable on the banner itself. Consider rewriting the banner to use a background tint with a leading icon instead when implementing the PHP template.
