#5104 Marcus Abernathy On-hold

Marcus's last renewal payment went through in Stripe on April 8. He was charged $29. But his subscription status stayed “on hold” instead of switching back to active.

What will change
Sub status on-hold → active
Renewal #5105 on-hold → completed
Next payment May 8 · 09:00 → May 8 · 09:00 (unchanged)

You can undo this from Fix history at any time. No payment will be taken.

Resolved in v1. Earlier /plan-eng-review pushed this warning to v1.1; on reconsideration it ships in v1. When the plugin detects that a fix's scheduled action has already executed (i.e., the payment ran and charged the customer), the modal above renders an explicit warning block - see $already_executed = true branch in v2-assets/admin/views/modal-fix-preview.php. Reverting will never silently claim to undo a charge.
